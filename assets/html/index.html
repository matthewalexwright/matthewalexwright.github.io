Conventions. Effective ionic radii are referenced to r(O²⁻, CN VI) = 1.40 Å; crystal radii to 1.26 Å — the two scales differ by a uniform 0.14 Å (cations larger, anions smaller on the crystal scale). Coordination geometry flags: SQ square-planar, PY pyramidal. HS/LS denote high- and low-spin configurations of octahedral (and some tetrahedral) transition-metal ions. Negative radii for H⁺/D⁺ and N⁵⁺ (CN III) reflect the proton or bare cation sitting inside the anion's electron cloud. Data as digitized in the pymatgen distribution of Shannon's Table 1.
