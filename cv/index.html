Professional Summary

I am a solid-state materials chemist working on battery and quantum materials. I study how structure governs function in inorganic solids to develop rational design principles for new functional materials. I combine solid-state synthesis with electrochemistry as a synthetic tool for tuning composition and electronic structure, and use X-ray and neutron scattering alongside spectroscopy to resolve the structural origins of ionic, electronic, and magnetic behavior.
