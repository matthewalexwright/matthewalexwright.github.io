I’ve received a $1,500 Heeger Fellowship from the California NanoSystems Institute (CNSI) and the Materials Research Laboratory (MRL) at UC Santa Barbara. The fellowship honors Nobel Laureate Professor Alan J. Heeger’s pioneering contributions to polymer and materials research, and it helps graduate students and postdoctoral researchers present their work at conferences and build their professional networks. I’ll be using it to attend the Spring meeting of the ACS in early 2027.

[You can read more about the Dr. Alan J. Heeger Fellowships here.](https://www.mrl.ucsb.edu/heeger-fellowships)
