UC awards $18 million to scale up the ambition and impact of AI in science. 
The University of California has awarded $6 million to our LEAP project (Low-Energy AI-Informed Phase Transitions) through its new AI Science at Scale initiative. Led by Professor Ram Seshadri at UC Santa Barbara, LEAP aims to accelerate discovery of low-energy switching materials by training specialized large language models on theory, experimental data, and simulations. By focusing on topological materials with unconventional electron behavior, our team seeks to identify promising candidates for energy-efficient chip design.
