UC awards $18 million towards AI Science at Scale. $6 million has been awarded to LEAP (Low-Energy AI-Informed Phase Transitions) which will be led by Professor Ram Seshadri at UC Santa Barbara, which aims to use specialized large language models in tandem with experimental researchers, to discover low-energy switching materials for more energy-efficient computing.
